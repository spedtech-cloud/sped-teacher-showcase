Check Before Sharing

Practise checking digital information and deciding whether to share.

What to do

Read each fictional online message, then choose a responsible next step. None of these messages are real and no sharing takes place.

For educators

Discuss why a step is safer, not only which button is correct. Teach learners to pause, check relevant primary sources or ask a trusted adult. Later practise with teacher-approved real digital information.

Fictional, offline teaching simulation. No personal information is requested, sent or stored on a server. On-screen practice does not by itself demonstrate independent use of a real device or service.
