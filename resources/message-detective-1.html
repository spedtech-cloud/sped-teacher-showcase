Message Detective · Teacher view

Three message situations · guided and independent pathways

Teaching note: Ask students to explore the message and decide what to do. Avoid prompting the safe answer. Observe sender checks, independent verification, help-seeking and response decisions. All contacts, messages, apps and outcomes are fictional. No messages or personal information are collected or transmitted. This view is not access-controlled; it is intended for the supervising educator.
